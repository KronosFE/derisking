Overview

The de-risking campaign

The published Tier-1 campaign — 141 validators frozen at the ratified point. The deeper hardening and concept tiers, the requirements traceability, and the full run register are in the Kronos team data room.

Kronos is not a rendering — it is a computed, published machine. Every performance claim traces to a first-principles calculation on our in-house HPC: negative-triangularity turbulence suppression (CGYRO), 99.98% alpha confinement (ASCOT5), tritium self-sufficiency (OpenMC), and a converged free-boundary equilibrium (FreeGS) — 141 validators, frozen 2026-08-27 and deposited with permanent DOIs. The core bet — that negative triangularity calms the turbulence which leaks heat — is confirmed across ion and electron scales and is robust to collisionality. What remains is engineering execution, now underway.
